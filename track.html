How to Draw the Pit Lane

1

Draw Pit Road: Draw the pit lane inside the track in the red area as an ordinary white road (~40px+ wide).

2

Leave a Wall: Keep a red wall barrier of about 15–25 px between the pit lane and the main track.

3

Connectors: Open the lane to the main track at both ends with white connector roads.

4

Pit Markers: Paint Green across the entry connector and Yellow across the exit connector edge to edge.
